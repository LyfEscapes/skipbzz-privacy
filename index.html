Privacy Policy — Skip & Bzz

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Skip & Bzz is made by t0ilet b0wl gaMez ("we"). We built this game for kids and families, so we keep it simple: we don't collect personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Game progress (acorns, gold stars, unlocked maps, sound on/off) is saved only on your device in local storage. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled entirely by Google Play (or itch.io on the web). We never see payment details. A parent gate (a math question) appears before any purchase screen.

Children

The game is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Internet use

The Android app works fully offline. Its fonts are bundled inside the app, so it makes no network requests of its own. Only Google Play makes requests, for purchases.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update it here and change the date above.
