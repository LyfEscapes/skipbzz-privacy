Skip & Bzz

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Android, iPhone and iPad · Effective November 26, 2026

Skip & Bzz ("the app") is a game for kids and families published by LyfEscapes LLC ("we", "us"). We keep it simple: the app does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

Game progress (acorns, gold stars, unlocked maps, sound on or off) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use your device's own backup service, this saved data may be included in that backup, which Apple or Google handles under your account.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad, through Google Play on Android, and through itch.io for the web version. The store takes the payment; we never see your name, card or account details. The app only learns whether the unlock is owned, so it can open the extra maps. A grown-up question (a multiplication problem) appears before any purchase screen.

Internet use

The game plays offline. Its art, sounds and fonts are inside the app, so it makes no network requests of its own. Only the App Store or Google Play connects, to show the price, make a purchase or restore one.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not ask for permission to track.

Children

The app is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws such as the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
