What Skip & Bzz is

A hand-drawn platformer about a boy named Skip and his bee, Bzz. There are no timers, no lives and no game overs. The first map, Around Home, is free. One purchase unlocks the other four maps: Out Yonder, Long Ago, Outer Space and Beach & Sea. The game plays offline.

How to play

  • Hold your device sideways. Tap a spot on the map to play it; tap a tab or swipe for more maps.
  • The arrows at the lower left move Skip. The round buttons at the lower right jump and buzz.
  • Collect acorns and find the gold-star sticker hidden in every level.
  • Tap the speaker at the top right to turn sound on or off.

Common problems

Blank screen. Close the app fully and open it again. If it stays blank, restart your device and install any update for the app and for your system. Don't delete the app first if you want to keep your progress.

No sound. Tap the speaker at the top right. Turn up the volume and check silent mode and any connected headphones.

Lost progress. Progress is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, so we can't see or recover it, and it doesn't move to another device through us. Deleting the app deletes its progress.

Unlocking and restoring

Open a locked map and tap Grown-ups: Unlock. A grown-up answers a multiplication question, then the store's own purchase sheet appears.

  • iPhone and iPad: sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases.
  • Android: use the Google account that made the purchase, open the unlock screen and tap Restore.

Purchases belong to the store where you bought them, so they don't move between Apple and Android devices. With Ask to Buy, the maps unlock once the family organiser approves.

Refunds

The store takes the payment and decides on refunds.

  • iPhone and iPad: request an Apple refund.
  • Android: request a Google Play refund.
